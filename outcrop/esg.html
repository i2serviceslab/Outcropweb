ESG & Sustainability

Environmental Stewardship, Community Value & Corporate Responsibility

Building Sustainable Value in Colombia

At Outcrop Silver, our commitment to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) excellence is fundamental to how we operate. As we advance our flagship Santa Ana primary silver project in the historic Mariquita district of Tolima, we integrate modern scientific exploration with genuine community partnership, environmental protection, and transparent corporate governance.

Environmental Stewardship

We are dedicated to minimizing our operational footprint and preserving Colombia's rich biodiversity throughout the project lifecycle.

  • Selective Underground Focus: Compact surface footprint with minimal environmental disturbance compared to open-pit operations.
  • Clean Metallurgy (Cyanide-Free): High silver and gold recoveries using simple gravity and flotation methods, eliminating the need for cyanide.
  • Water & Soil Stewardship: Rigorous baseline monitoring of local waterways and proactive erosion controls across all active drill platforms.

Social Value & Community

Sustainable exploration requires genuine, respectful collaboration with local communities, landowners, and regional authorities.

  • Local Employment Priority: Over 90% of our on-site operational team and field workforce are hired directly from Falan, Mariquita, and nearby towns.
  • Community Dialogue: Active, ongoing engagement with Community Action Boards (JACs), local farmers, and municipal leaders.
  • Local Infrastructure & Well-being: Continuous investment in local road maintenance, community center support, and educational programs.

Governance & Ethics

We adhere to the highest standards of transparency, corporate integrity, and regulatory accountability in Canada and Colombia.

  • Board-Level ESG Oversight: Specialized board members including Ana Milena Vásquez, dedicated to ESG and social responsibility.
  • Anti-Corruption & Transparency: Strict adherence to Canadian TSX guidelines and Colombian mining compliance regulations.
  • Robust Policies: Enforced policies on Business Conduct, Whistleblower protection, and Audit Committee governance.
