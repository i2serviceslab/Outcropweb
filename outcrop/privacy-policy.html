Privacy Policy

Commitment to Data Protection & Confidentiality

1. Introduction

Outcrop Silver & Gold Corporation ("Outcrop Silver", "the Company", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our website visitors, investors, and stakeholders. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information obtained through our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Subscribe to our corporate newsletter, news releases, or investor email alerts.
  • Submit inquiries via our contact forms or email addresses.
  • Request corporate presentations, financial reports, or marketing materials.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any other details you choose to provide. In addition, our web servers automatically collect standard technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited, to improve site performance and security.

3. Use of Information

The information collected is used strictly for legitimate business and corporate purposes, including:

  • Distributing news releases, exploration updates, and corporate announcements.
  • Responding to inquiries, comments, or requests from shareholders and the public.
  • Analyzing website traffic and usage trends to enhance user experience and site navigation.
  • Fulfilling legal, regulatory, and compliance requirements applicable to publicly listed issuers.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

Outcrop Silver does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers (such as email delivery platforms and website hosting partners) solely to the extent necessary to deliver services on our behalf, under strict confidentiality obligations.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to facilitate website navigation, personalize your experience, and compile aggregate statistical data. You may modify your browser settings to decline cookies or to notify you when a cookie is placed.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Unsubscribe and Contact

You may unsubscribe from our email distribution lists at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link provided at the bottom of our emails or by contacting our corporate office directly.
